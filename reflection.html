Work Reflection – Several Years of Experience

Over the past several years in my professional life, I’ve experienced steady growth in both my spiritual development and my work experience. Each year brought new challenges, responsibilities, and opportunities that shaped the way I think, work, and make decisions. Looking back, I can see how the principles I’ve tried to live by helped me become more intentional, more disciplined, and more focused on following Jesus Christ in every part of my life. As Elder Bednar taught, “all things gather in Christ,” and I’ve felt that truth as my spiritual and professional growth became more connected (Bednar, 2018).

My spiritual growth began with simple efforts to strengthen Christlike attributes, but over time it became deeper and more meaningful. I learned to recognize when I was drifting away from spiritual focus and how to realign myself through small, consistent habits. Moments of temple reflection gave me peace and direction, reminding me that when my spiritual life is clear, my professional life becomes clearer too. Casey Hurley’s reminder that “faith and effort bring real growth” helped me stay motivated during difficult seasons at work (Hurley, n.d.). Through these experiences, I became more aware of how my spiritual habits influence my thoughts, my choices, and the way I serve others.

Professionally, my years of work helped me understand how ethics, responsible decision‑making, and thoughtful design connect to my identity as a disciple of Jesus Christ. Facing ethical situations taught me how to make honest and responsible choices, even when the situation was complicated. Working with users, clients, and colleagues showed me the value of listening, patience, and improving systems based on real feedback. Year after year, I gained more confidence in my technical abilities while staying grounded in strong values. Elder Morrison’s message that “the Lord guides us through unexpected paths” helped me trust the process, even when work didn’t go the way I expected (Morrison, 2022).

One of the most important lessons I’ve learned is that faith and technology can work together. Spiritual principles like honesty, patience, humility, and compassion are not separate from my IT work. They influence how I troubleshoot problems, how I communicate with others, and how I treat people. As I reflect on my projects and experiences over the years, I can clearly see how my spiritual and professional growth strengthened each other.

As I continue moving forward in my career, I feel more prepared to grow as a capable and trusted disciple in my field. I understand my strengths better, I know where I need to improve, and I have a clearer vision of the kind of professional I want to become. These past years have given me a strong foundation that I can carry into my future work, my service, and my personal life.
